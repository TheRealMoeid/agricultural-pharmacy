- **Admin simplified.** No price field. Habibollah's frequent updates are now just stock status, plus occasional product edits.
- **Product record now includes:** trade name, generic (active-ingredient) name, type, usage, dosage, timing, photo, manufacturer, importer, country of origin, production date, expiry date, available/unavailable.
- **Expert profiles:** Rahmatollah shows degree, experience, and role. Habibollah shows degree and specialty only (no experience).
- **Launch scope:** articles are not needed at launch, but the site should have room for a seasonal articles section.
- **Main goal of the site:** more sales. Since customers order by phone, calling should be the easiest action on every page.
